Interpretation

What this model can—and cannot—say

The classifier estimates next-session close-to-close direction using expanding walk-forward validation. It combines price action, cross-market risk, volatility, macro proxies, breadth, credit conditions, and a bounded news overlay. Short-horizon direction remains noisy; probabilities are estimates, not guarantees or investment advice.
